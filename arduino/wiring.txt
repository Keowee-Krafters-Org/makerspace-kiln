# Kiln Controller Wiring (Text Version)

## Pin Connections

### MAX31855 Thermocouple Amplifier
TinyDuino SAMD (Host)    <-->    Adafruit MAX31855 Breakout
-------------------------------------------------------
GND                      <-->    GND
VCC (3.3V or 5V)         <-->    Vin
Pin 5                    <-->    CLK
Pin 4                    <-->    CS
Pin 3                    <-->    DO

### Thermocouple
Thermocouple (Type K)    <-->    MAX31855 Terminal Block
-------------------------------------------------------
Yellow Wire (+)          <-->    +
Red Wire (-)             <-->    -

### Solid State Relays (SSRs)
TinyDuino SAMD (Host)    <-->    SSR Control Terminals
-------------------------------------------------------
Pin 6                    <-->    Upper SSR Control (+)
Pin 7                    <-->    Lower SSR Control (+)
GND                      <-->    SSR Control (-) (Both)
